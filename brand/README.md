# Brand

The mark is a dorsal fin leaning into the direction of travel. It is one solid
shape with no inner detail, because the smallest place it appears is a 24 pixel
tab icon and anything finer turns to noise there.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `flowfin-avatar.svg` / `.png` | Organisation and social avatars. White on blue. |
| `flowfin-dark.svg` | Placement on a dark surface. Accent blue on near black. |
| `flowfin-light.svg` | Placement on a light surface. Deep blue on white. |
| `flowfin-mono.svg` | Single colour, inherits `currentColor`. For inline use. |

## Colour

`#0A6CE8` on light grounds, `#5B9CFF` on dark. These are the accent values from
the design system, so the mark and the interface agree rather than merely
coordinate.

Blue against white or near black separates by luminance, not by hue. That is
what makes it hold for protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia alike, and it is
why the mark carries no second colour.

## Clear space and size

Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the tile width on every side. Do not
place the mark on a photograph, do not outline it, do not add a shadow, and do
not rotate it. Below 24 pixels use the avatar tile rather than the bare mark.
